Tumble privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · App: Tumble (com.lazycode.tumble) · Developer: Lazycode

Tumble is a live wallpaper with tilt-controlled mini games. We built it so that nothing about you ever leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Tumble does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device data. The app has no internet permission, so it is technically unable to send data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

  • Your chosen game and character.
  • Your best scores.

These are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling Tumble deletes them.

Motion sensor

The games are played by tilting your phone, so Tumble reads the gravity / motion sensor while the wallpaper is visible. Sensor readings are used only to move your character in that moment. They are never recorded or stored.

Ads, analytics and accounts

Tumble shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, and needs no account or sign-in.

Purchases

If Tumble ever offers an optional in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Children

Tumble does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
