NetSpeed privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 (speed test) · App: NetSpeed Indicator (com.netspeed.indicator) · Developer: Lazycode (Soumya Ranjan Sethy) · Contact: sethy.soumyaranjan@gmail.com · Website: netspeed.lazycode.ai

NetSpeed shows your live internet speed and, when you ask, tests it. We built it so that nothing about you ever leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. NetSpeed does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal or device data. We have no server, account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The speed meter reads your phone's own counters and never uses the network.

The speed test (since version 1.4.0) is the only thing that uses the internet (android.permission.INTERNET), and only while a test you started is running. It downloads and uploads throw-away test data to and from Cloudflare's public speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com) and measures how fast that goes. Like any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address and the test traffic while the test runs; its handling is covered by the Cloudflare privacy policy. NetSpeed sends nothing else: no identifiers, no usage data, no results. Your last 10 results (speeds, ping, time and server city) are kept only on your phone.

A test uses about 50–300 MB of data depending on your speed; on mobile data the app asks before it starts.

What the app reads

  • Total bytes sent and received by the device, from Android's TrafficStats counters, once per second, to show the speed. These are byte totals only: no websites, app names or content.
  • Connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data) and signal level, to label the reading.

NetSpeed does not read your browsing, messages, files, contacts, location, clipboard or the contents of any traffic.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: display mode, scene and theme, colours, fonts, bubble size and position, speed-tier names and thresholds, daily data cap.
  • Your usage totals: today, lifetime, and a rolling 30-day history.

These are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling NetSpeed deletes them.

Fonts

Some on-screen typefaces are provided by the Google Play services font provider already on your phone. NetSpeed asks that on-device service for a font by name; it sends no personal or usage data.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEKeeps one lightweight service running so the speed stays live. In status-bar mode Android shows a notification, which is where the live icon is drawn.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows that notification (status-bar mode only).
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOWDraws the optional floating speed bubble next to the camera. Decline it and the app works in status-bar mode.
INTERNETOnly for the speed test you start (test data to and from Cloudflare's speed-test service). The meter never uses it.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEWi-Fi vs mobile data, the Wi-Fi signal level, and a warning before a speed test on mobile data.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRestarts the meter after a reboot.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONSOnly if you choose to exempt the app from battery limits, so the reading isn't stopped. Never requested automatically.

Ads, analytics and accounts

NetSpeed shows no ads, contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, needs no account, and has no in-app purchases.

Children

NetSpeed is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
