NetSpeed privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 (usage and crash data, optional sign-in) · App: NetSpeed Indicator (com.netspeed.indicator) · Developer: Lazycode (Soumya Ranjan Sethy) · Contact: sethy.soumyaranjan@gmail.com · Website: netspeed.lazycode.ai

NetSpeed shows your live internet speed and, when you ask, tests it. It collects no personal data such as your name, email, location or contacts. You can use every feature without an account; signing in with Google is optional. If you allow it, it sends anonymous usage and crash data, described below.

What we collect

The meter and your settings stay on your phone. The speed meter reads your phone's own counters and never uses the network. Your settings, your usage totals and your last 10 speed-test results are kept only in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall.

Usage and crash data (optional). NetSpeed uses Google Firebase: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring, to see where setup gets stuck, fix crashes and keep the app fast. When this is on, NetSpeed sends:

  • Usage events: which screens and sections of the app are opened and in what order, how long each is on screen, which options you change (for example "display mode: bubble", never a colour or a file), permission prompts and their result (granted, denied), whether the meter kept running and how often Android restarted it (counts and ranges, once a day), speed-test starts, stops and failures, and the speed test's result as ranges only (for example "download 25 to 100 Mbps"), and the sort of connection it ran on (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • Crash reports and performance data: the crash's technical details, and how long the app takes to start and draw its screens. No network timing is collected.
  • Identifiers and device details: a random app-instance identifier made by Firebase; whether the app was installed from Google Play or is a test build; your phone's brand, model and Android version, the app version and language; and a coarse region and city that Google works out from your IP address when the data arrives. We do not receive or keep your IP address. Install referrer: Google Play tells the app which Play page or campaign led to the install. If you sign in, these reports also carry a one-way code made from your account ID, not from your name or email. We can match it to your account, for example to contact you about a crash.

What this never includes: the speeds your connection measured (only ranges of a speed-test result), Wi-Fi or network names, IP addresses, web addresses, the speed-test server, anything on your screen, your location, your name, email or phone number (usage and crash data never carries them; they exist only if you sign in, see below), contacts, files, or the advertising ID. NetSpeed does not use the advertising ID and has no ads.

You choose. One switch, Share usage and crash data (home page, section 14, "Privacy"), turns all three Firebase services on or off. It is on by default for new installs, except in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where NetSpeed cannot tell where you are, where it stays off until NetSpeed asks you once and you agree. Nothing is collected before that. Turning it off stops collection at once. If you were already using NetSpeed before this change, nothing is collected until you have seen a notice on the home page ("NetSpeed now collects usage and crash data to improve the app. You can turn this off in Privacy."); after you close it, sharing is on unless you have switched it off. Usage data without an account carries only a random identifier that changes when you reinstall or clear the app's data, so we cannot tell which data is yours; it is removed when Google's retention period ends. To ask about your data, write to sethy.soumyaranjan@gmail.com.

The speed test (since version 1.4.0) is the only thing that uses the internet for NetSpeed's own purposes (android.permission.INTERNET), and only while a test you started is running. It downloads and uploads throw-away test data to and from Cloudflare's public speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com) and measures how fast that goes. Like any website, Cloudflare sees your IP address and the test traffic while the test runs; its handling is covered by the Cloudflare privacy policy. NetSpeed sends nothing else to Cloudflare. Your last 10 results (speeds, ping, time and server city) are kept only on your phone. With "Share usage and crash data" on, only a range of the result, such as "download 25 to 100 Mbps", goes into usage data, never the exact numbers or the server.

A test uses about 50–300 MB of data depending on your speed; on mobile data the app asks before it starts.

What the app reads

  • Total bytes sent and received by the device, from Android's TrafficStats counters, once per second, to show the speed. These are byte totals only: no websites, app names or content.
  • Connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile data) and signal level, to label the reading.

NetSpeed does not read your browsing, messages, files, contacts, location, clipboard or the contents of any traffic.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings: display mode, scene and theme, colours, fonts, bubble size and position, speed-tier names and thresholds, daily data cap, your Share usage and crash data choice and your sign-in offer state.
  • Your usage totals: today, lifetime, and a rolling 30-day history.
  • If you sign in, the Firebase sign-in session, until you sign out.

These are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling NetSpeed deletes them.

Fonts

Some on-screen typefaces are provided by the Google Play services font provider already on your phone. NetSpeed asks that on-device service for a font by name; it sends no personal or usage data.

Signing in (optional)

You can use NetSpeed without an account, and every feature works the same signed out. If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your name and email address, held in Firebase Authentication with the account's creation and last sign-in time. We use them to run your account, for support, to contact you about problems such as crashes, for internal reporting and analysis, and for email from Lazycode as described below. Our own systems can read them for these purposes. We never sell them. NetSpeed's settings, usage totals and speed-test history are never part of your account and stay on your phone.

Email: outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland, signing in means you agree we may email you about your account and with news from Lazycode, and the sign-in sheet says so. Inside those regions, or where we cannot tell, NetSpeed asks and sends nothing without a Yes. You can turn it off any time under Privacy, Account. The choice, its basis, time and policy version are stored in Firestore in a record only your account can read or write through the app.

Deleting: Privacy, Account, Delete account, on the web at netspeed.lazycode.ai/delete-account/, or email soumya@lazycode.ai. Signing out deletes nothing. Deleting removes your account and your email choice.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEKeeps one lightweight service running so the speed stays live. In status-bar mode Android shows a notification, which is where the live icon is drawn.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShows that notification (status-bar mode only).
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOWDraws the optional floating speed bubble next to the camera. Decline it and the app works in status-bar mode.
INTERNETThe speed test you start (test data to and from Cloudflare's speed-test service), and, if you allow it, the usage and crash data above, sent to Google. The meter itself never uses it.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATEWi-Fi vs mobile data, the Wi-Fi signal level, and a warning before a speed test on mobile data.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRestarts the meter after a reboot.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONSOnly if you choose to exempt the app from battery limits, so the reading isn't stopped. Never requested automatically.
WAKE_LOCKAdded by Google's libraries so usage and crash data can be sent in the background. NetSpeed does not use it to keep your phone awake.
com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICELets Google Play tell the app which Play page or campaign led to the install, for the usage data above.
com.google.android.providers.gsf.permission.READ_GSERVICESAdded by Google's sign-in and Firebase libraries so they can read Google Play services settings. NetSpeed does not use it for anything else.

Ads, analytics and accounts

NetSpeed shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and uses no advertising ID. It contains Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring, described above, behind the Share usage and crash data switch, and Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for the optional sign-in, described above.

Children

NetSpeed is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data unless a person chooses to sign in with Google. The anonymous usage and crash data above is not aimed at children and can be switched off.
